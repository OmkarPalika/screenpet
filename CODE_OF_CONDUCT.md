# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are trying.

## What that means here

This project has one maintainer and, mostly, strangers who found it. That makes
the tone entirely a matter of what people choose to do, so:

- **Criticise the code, not the person.** "This breaks on a null window handle"
  is a review. "Did you even test this" is not.
- **Assume competence and good faith.** Someone filing a confused bug report has
  usually hit a real problem and described it badly. Ask what they saw.
- **Take a correction well.** Being wrong in public is the ordinary cost of
  working in the open, and nobody who admits it should be made to regret it.
- **Not everyone here shares your first language.** Read for what was meant.

## Not acceptable

Harassment of any kind. Insults or demeaning comments, including about someone's
age, disability, appearance, ethnicity, gender identity or expression,
experience level, nationality, race, religion, or sexual identity or
orientation. Sexualised language or imagery. Publishing anyone's private
information without their permission. Sustained disruption of a discussion.
Threats, whether or not you meant them.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## What happens

Report it to palikaomkar@gmail.com with "screenpet conduct" in the subject.
Reports are read by the maintainer and kept private. Say what happened and link
to it if it is public.

The response depends on what happened, and escalates: a private word, a public
correction, having a comment or commit removed, a temporary block, or a
permanent one. Deliberate harassment does not get a first warning.

If the person you need to report is the maintainer, say so in the subject line —
that is a fair thing to have to write and you will get a straight answer.

## Scope, honestly

This is not a large community, and there is no committee behind it. What you get
is one person reading the mail and trying to be fair. If you want the fuller,
lawyer-reviewed treatment of these ideas, the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
is the standard text and nothing above is meant to contradict it.
