screenpet

Everything stays on this machine.

Used for reading text off the screen.

For example CommandOrControl+Shift+Space.

Uses a voice already installed on Windows. Mute from the tray icon.

Adds Listen… to the pet's menu. Windows recognises the speech on this machine; no audio is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Needs the setting above. This holds the microphone open for as long as it is on — that is what a wake word costs, and there is no version of it that does not. Windows does the listening, on this machine, with a grammar containing only the wake phrases: it is not a transcriber looking for a word, it is incapable of recognising anything else. The only thing that ever comes back from it is the fact that you said it.

Needs the microphone too, and holds it open. The pet listens for a beat and moves on it. The audio is turned into one number — how loud, right now — and thrown away; nothing is recognised, recorded or sent. Off, it still dances when you ask, and opens the microphone only for the length of the dance.

Uses the camera to tell whether anything moved, and nothing else. Frames are shrunk to 32×24, compared with the one before, and discarded — it cannot recognise a face, and nothing is saved or sent. A green dot on the pet shows whenever the camera is open. Ask it to take a photo and it keeps that one frame, in your Pictures folder — the only frame it ever writes down, and still nothing it sends anywhere.

Needs the camera. Uses the face detector built into Windows, so the pet can tell the difference between a person sitting down and a door opening. It answers how many faces and nothing else — no name, no identity, no template kept. The frame is handed to Windows in memory and never written to disk.

The only setting that sends anything off this machine. With it on, asking about the weather sends the town below to open-meteo.com — no account, no key, nothing from your screen, camera or microphone, and no location lookup. Off, the pet says it cannot see outside, which is the truth.

The only thing that ever leaves. You can put the next town over.

Say remember my standup is at 9 and it writes that down, on this machine, and brings it up later. Only what you tell it to remember is written — never your chat, your screen, the microphone or the camera. Everything else it picks up is a counter: which hour you tend to ask it things, how often you looked after it today. Say what do you remember to read it back, forget the standup to drop one, or forget everything to empty it. Switching this off deletes the file.

Needs the setting above. The pet occasionally brings up what it remembers to needle you — how many headpats you managed on your best day against today, the hour you always turn up, the thing you told it you would do. Every one of those numbers is real; it teases with what it has, not with anything invented. At most one remark every ninety minutes, and never during quiet hours.
